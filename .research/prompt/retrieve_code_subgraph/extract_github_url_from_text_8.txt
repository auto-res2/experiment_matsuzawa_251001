# Paper Outline
The methodology leverages MLMC to approximate analytically intractable multi-step look-ahead acquisition functions formulated as Markov Decision Processes (MDPs). It uses a nested Sample Average Approximation (SAA) to handle nested optimization and Monte Carlo (MC) estimations. MLMC constructs a telescoping sum of estimators from low to high accuracy, performing most simulations with low-accuracy and low-cost to reduce overall computational complexity. An antithetic coupling approach is introduced to further improve the convergence rate. The approach is applied to approximate the maximizer of the acquisition function directly, as well as the acquisition function itself (for theoretical analysis), within a Gaussian Process-based BO framework.
