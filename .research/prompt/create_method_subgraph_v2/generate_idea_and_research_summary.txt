    "Methodology": "DyHPO employs a Gaussian Process (GP) surrogate model with a novel deep kernel. This deep kernel, denoted as K(θ, w), uses a neural network φ as a feature extractor. The network φ takes the hyperparameter configuration, the past learning curve, and the current budget as input, processing them through linear and 1D convolutional layers followed by global max pooling, before feeding to a squared exponential kernel k.
